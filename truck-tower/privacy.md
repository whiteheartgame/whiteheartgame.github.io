# Truck Tower — Gizlilik Politikası

*Son güncelleme: 31 Ekim 2026*

Bu politika, Truck Tower mobil oyununun ("Oyun") hangi bilgileri nasıl kullandığını açıklar.

## Topladığımız bilgiler
- **Oyun ilerlemesi:** bölüm, kule yüksekliği, rekor ve ayarlar (ses, titreşim, dil, reklam tercihi) yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır, bize gönderilmez.
- **Reklam verileri:** Oyun ücretsizdir ve reklamlarla desteklenir. Reklamlar Google AdMob tarafından gösterilir. AdMob şu bilgileri otomatik olarak işler:
  - cihazın reklam kimliği ve uygulama grubu kimliği
  - IP adresi ve buna dayalı yaklaşık konum (ülke ya da şehir düzeyinde)
  - cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi, dil ve ağ bilgileri
  - reklam etkileşimleri (örneğin bir reklamın gösterilmesi, izlenmesi ya da tıklanması)

  Bu bilgiler reklam göstermek, reklamların performansını ölçmek ve sahte tıklamaları önlemek için kullanılır. Veriler aktarım sırasında şifrelenir.

Adınızı, e-posta adresinizi, telefon numaranızı ya da kişisel iletişim bilgilerinizi toplamayız. Oyunda hesap ya da giriş yoktur. Bizim bir sunucumuz yoktur; bu bilgiler bize ulaşmaz, doğrudan Google'a gider.

## Üçüncü taraflar
- Google AdMob (Google LLC ve bağlı şirketleri) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Seçenekleriniz
- **Türkiye:** Oyun ilk açıldığında kişiselleştirilmiş reklam için izniniz sorulur. İzin vermezseniz oyun aynen oynanır, reklamlar kişiselleştirilmez. Seçiminizi istediğiniz zaman **Ayarlar → Reklam tercihi** bölümünden değiştirebilirsiniz.
- **Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre:** reklam kişiselleştirmesi için Google'ın izin formu gösterilir. Seçiminizi **Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri** bölümünden değiştirebilirsiniz.
- Cihazınızın ayarlarından reklam kimliğinizi sıfırlayabilir ya da silebilirsiniz (Android: Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar).
- Oyunu cihazınızdan kaldırdığınızda cihazdaki bütün oyun verileri silinir.

# Truck Tower — Privacy Policy (English)

*Last updated: 31 October 2026*

This policy explains what information the Truck Tower mobile game ("the Game") uses and how.

## Information we collect
- **Game progress:** level, tower height, best score and settings (sound, vibration, language, ad choice) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
- **Ad data:** the Game is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which automatically processes:
  - your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  - your IP address and the approximate (country or city level) location derived from it
  - device model, operating system, language and network information
  - ad interactions (for example an ad being shown, watched or clicked)

  This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance and prevent invalid clicks. Data is encrypted in transit.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other contact details. The Game has no accounts or sign-in. We have no servers; this information never reaches us and goes directly to Google.

## Third parties
- Google AdMob (Google LLC and its affiliates) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Your choices
- **Türkiye:** the first time you open the Game, you are asked for consent to personalized ads. If you decline, the Game works exactly the same and ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Ad choice**.
- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** Google's consent form is shown for ad personalization. You can change your choice under **Settings → Privacy options**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
- Uninstalling the Game deletes all game data stored on your device.

## Users in Türkiye: notice under Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK)

**Data controller:** Yusuf Samet Akyürek (independent game developer)
**Contact:** whiteheartgame@gmail.com

**Personal data processed:**
- advertising ID and app set ID;
- IP address and approximate (country or city level) location;
- device model, operating system, language and network information;
- ad interactions.

Game progress and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. This data is collected by Google AdMob's software inside the Game and sent directly to Google.

**Purposes and legal bases:**
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Personalized ads (ads chosen based on your interests) | Your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1): "Allow" in the consent window shown when you first open the Game |
| Showing non-personalized ads so the Game can be free, measuring ad delivery, preventing invalid clicks | Legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f) |

If you decline, ads are not personalized and are chosen only from the Game's content; no part of the Game depends on your consent.

**Transfers abroad:**
- Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA, and its affiliates). The data above is processed on Google's servers in the USA and other countries.
- Transfers for personalized ads are based on the explicit consent you give in the consent window (KVKK art. 9/6-a).
- **Possible risks:** these countries may not protect personal data to the same level as Türkiye; foreign laws may apply to your data and it may be harder to exercise your rights.

**Consent record:** your choice in the consent window, its date and an identifier of the text you saw are stored only on your device.

**Retention:**
- Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.
- Ad data processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention periods: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

**Your rights (KVKK art. 11):** By contacting us, you can:
- learn whether your data is processed;
- request information about the processing;
- learn its purpose and whether it is used accordingly;
- know the third parties it is transferred to, in Türkiye or abroad;
- request correction;
- request deletion or destruction under art. 7;
- request that these actions be notified to those third parties;
- object to a result against you arising from automated analysis;
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

**How to apply:**
- Send your request to whiteheartgame@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days.
- Since most of this data reaches Google rather than us, resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) is the fastest route.
- You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
